# Artwork prompts

These workshop paintings were created with the built-in image-generation tool for TatsunaKyo's art direction. The lettering and emblem are separate SVG assets, exported with the project script. No game screenshot or official logo was used as an input.

## Moon workshop concept

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ORIGINAL cinematic GitHub README banner background, one horizontal image approximately 1800 x 700 pixels, no typography yet. An intimate dark Gothic Victorian hunter's workshop. On the RIGHT half a solitary hunter is visibly STANDING upright before a worn wooden workbench: feet and standing posture must be clear, not seated, not crouching. The hunter wears a restrained dark worn hunting coat with leather and a brimmed hat, quietly examining a single intricate mechanical hunting tool on the bench. A warm antique lantern illuminates the bench and the hunter's hands. Just a few arranged hunting tools and believable workshop details, uncluttered. Behind the hunter and bench on the far right, a tall Gothic arched window frames an ENORMOUS pale dusty-red moon, filling most of the window, softly mottled, with a cool charcoal-blue night beyond. The moon is pale and desaturated, not a fiery saturated red sun. Original scene in the atmosphere of Bloodborne, without copying an existing screenshot, official logo, weapon design or promotional artwork. LEFT 45 percent: quiet deeply shadowed stone wall with subtle Gothic architectural framing, dark negative space reserved for legible text and a reusable emblem later. No major subject on the left. Right 55 percent: workshop, standing hunter, warm lantern and window. Rich painterly realism, subtle engraved textures, restrained cinematic lighting, intimate and mysterious. Charcoal, aged ivory, muted blue-gray and tarnished brass. Original, purposeful composition, no generic fantasy armor or sword-wielding hero. No text, letters, logo, watermark, particles, fog sparkles, excessive red light, glowing text or overcrowded weapons. This will become the personal identity banner of Bloodborne: The Crowned Workshop by TatsunaKyo.

## Lantern workshop concept

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ORIGINAL artwork background for a cinematic GitHub README banner, 1800 pixels wide and approximately 700 pixels tall. Create one wide horizontal illustration, not a mockup or collage. A dark, intimate Gothic hunter's workshop: a solitary Victorian hunter stands quietly with their back and profile to us at a worn workbench, inspecting an intricate mechanical hunting tool. Restrained practical details: a few arranged tools, cloth, carved wood, aged iron. Warm amber lantern light falls gently on the hunter's hands and table. Through a tall Gothic arched window on the right is an enormous pale-red moon, softly mottled and desaturated, against a cold charcoal-blue night. The atmosphere should belong in the universe of Bloodborne, but be an original scene, never copying a screenshot, official logo or promotional artwork. Composition for an eventual editable typography overlay: leave the LEFT 43 percent as deeply shadowed quiet wall and negative space, keep important artwork on the RIGHT 57 percent. The hunter and lamp near the center-right; the arched moonlit window at the right. Cinematic horizontal composition with elegant, believable perspective. Painterly realistic game concept art with tangible materials and controlled detail, atmospheric, intimate and mysterious, understated not flashy. Palette: charcoal, muted blue-gray, worn ivory, warm antique brass; moon a restrained pale dusty red. NO text, letters, logo, emblem or watermark anywhere. NO floating particles, no glowing text, no excessive red lighting, no crowded weapons wall, no generic fantasy warrior armor, no spectacle or magical effects. Output should be suitable as the background of 'Bloodborne: The Crowned Workshop', TatsunaKyo's personal workshop identity.
